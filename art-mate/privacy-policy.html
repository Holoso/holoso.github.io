Information Collection and Use

Information that we collect:

  • Approximate location by masked IP address to provide appropriate advertisement
  • Device metadata for analytics purposes
  • Advertising ID to provide appropriate advertisement
  • App-instance ID (this identifier is not associated with a user identifier) for analytics purposes
  • Stack traces, app lifecycle events, relevant device metadata and application state, diagnostic information used for analytics to collect data about how users use the app or how it performs
  • Which features you use and how many sketches you have, for analytics purposes (never the sketches themselves)

We use Crashlytics service in our applications. Please refer to their Privacy Policy.

We use Firebase analytics in our applications. Please refer to this page.

We use AdMob advertising service in our applications. Please refer also to this page for additional information.

The app downloads Challenges, Prompts of the Day and Picked Arts from our server, and their images from the websites that host them. Like any website, these servers see your IP address.

We do not automatically collect any personal information.

We do not share user data with other companies and organizations, apart from the Google services named above.

Your Sketches

Sketches you add with the camera or from your photos stay on your device, and so do your Favorites and Reminders. Photos you take with the camera are also saved to an Art Mate album in your photo library. Reminders are set by your device itself. We never receive any of this.

Backup

Backup keeps a copy of your sketches and Favorites in your own cloud storage, so you don't lose them if you change or lose your phone. It stays off until you turn it on. The copy belongs to you: it is never sent to us, and we cannot see it.

On iPhone and iPad, the copy is kept in the app's own space in your iCloud, under your Apple ID. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to it.

On Android, the copy is kept in your Google Drive, in a hidden folder only Art Mate can reach. The app cannot see any other files there. It keeps only your Google account's email address, on your device, to show which account Backup uses, and it never uses your Backup or email for ads or analytics, or lets anyone read them. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to it.

Art Mate's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

Deleting the app deletes everything it keeps on your device and stops all data collection. Photos in your photo library stay there.

Turning Backup off stops new copies. The copy already in your cloud stays until you delete it:

  • iCloud: open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage. Choose Art Mate and delete its data.
  • Google Drive: on drive.google.com, open Settings, then Manage apps. Next to Art Mate, choose Options, then Delete hidden app data. To take away Art Mate's access to your Drive, remove it from your Google Account's third-party connections.

Google keeps the analytics and crash data described above for a limited time and then deletes it, as explained in Privacy and Security in Firebase. This data is not linked to your name or email address, so we cannot look it up for one person.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iPhone and iPad, the app asks before tracking you, and you can change your answer in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
